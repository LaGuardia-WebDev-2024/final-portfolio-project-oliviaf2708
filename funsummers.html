Summer University Theatre Experience

SUTE is a program I did this past summer in 2025. At the start of summer I flew out to LA and was housed at Loyola Marymount University for the duration of the program. I had the opportunity to work with renowned faculty and proffesors from all over the country. Everything from scene study and monolauge work with USC, to movement classes with Carnegie Mellon. This program set me up so well to go into the college audion process, and made me feel more comfortable knowing I already knew someone in the audition room. I met the most amazing people and had one of the most fun experiences of my life.

UCLA On Screen Acting Program

This was a program I did two summers ago in 2024. I was able to stay on the UCLA campus while taking classes with their theatre program. I had such a fun experience writing and filming a scene with the frineds I met, and I learned so much about the technicals and skills to on camera acting. I loved being able to explore LA with my roomates, and get my first feel of what it was like to live on a college campus. It was truly an amazing experience.
