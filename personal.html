UCLA On Screen Acting Program

This was a program I did two summers ago in 2024. I was able to stay on the UCLA campus while taking classes with their theatre program. I had such a fun experience writing and filming a scene with the frineds I met, and I learned so much about the technicals and skills to on camera acting. I loved being able to explore LA with my roomates, and get my first feel of what it was like to live on a college campus. It was truly an amazing experience.
