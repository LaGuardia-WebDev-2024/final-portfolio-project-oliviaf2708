Winter 2025 Drama Cabaret

This was a project I worked on winter of my junior year in 2025. It was the first cabaret I'd even done, and I couldn't have asked for a better experience. This is an image of me singing Minor by Gracie Abrams at the Don't Tell Mama theater in Midtown.

Spring 2025 Drama Cabaret

This was the second cabaret I did my junior year at Chelsea Table. I collaborated with one of my friends and we performed We Don't Talk Anymore by Charlie Puth. I had such a fun time performing and chearing on all the other acts of stage.

Newport Jazz Festival

This past summer I had the amazing opportunity to attend Newport Jazz Camp. I worked alongside some amazing jazz musicians to put together a couple pieces of music to eventually perform at Newport Jazz Fest! I learned so much from my time at this camp thanks to the amazing students and faculty.
