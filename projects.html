Projects @ LAG

Below are some of the scenes, shows, and other performances I've worked on over my four years.

Senior Year

On the left is my role of ensamble in Urinetown! This show was my 2026 SDF and the first musical I had ever performed. I had such a wonderful time working with the cast and crew.It was my final, and most memorable performance at Laugardia. On the right is me in the role of Juror 8 in an adapted version of 12 Angry Men. This was for Davidson's One Acts in Fall 2025. I got the privelage to work with one of my best friends who directed the show.

Junior Year

On the left is my performance as Stella in A Streetcar Named Desire. I performed this as a part of my 2024 Winter Scenes. This was by far my favorite scene that I've done so far. I had the pleasure of workin with Nadia Eisner(who played Blanche), and my studio director, Jeffery Stafford. On the right is my Spring Sing performance where I performed All Falls Down from the musical Chaplin. I had an amazing time working working on this piece, and an even better time performing it. I got to work again with Jeffery Stafford as my musical theatre director, and Larry Presgrove as my musical director.
